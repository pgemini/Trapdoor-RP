How it works

Guarding the LLM's entrance.

Trapdoor sits between user-uploaded content and your model. Nothing gets through unscanned.

01

Ingest

Accept PDFs, images, videos, audio transcripts, and rich documents from any upload path.

02

Extract

Text layers, OCR, metadata, EXIF, frame-by-frame vision, and audio-to-text channels.

03

Detect

AI Foundry classifiers + heuristic rules score every fragment for injection intent.

04

Sanitize

Strip, quote, or block. Only trusted, neutralized context is passed to the LLM.
